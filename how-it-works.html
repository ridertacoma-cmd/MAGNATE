How Magnate Works

From first browse to closed deal. Everything happens inside Magnate.

FOR BUYERS
Find and acquire your next cashflow business
1
Browse the Feed

Scroll through a curated social-style feed of vetted cashflow businesses. Filter by type, revenue, margin, and asking price. Save deals to your watchlist.

2
Get AI Matched

Our AI learns your acquisition criteria and proactively surfaces deals that match your budget, preferred business type, and timeline. The more you use it the smarter it gets.

3
Connect with Sellers

Message verified sellers directly inside the app. Request financials, sign NDAs, ask questions, and negotiate — all in one place with a full conversation history.

4
Close with Escrow

Once you agree on terms we facilitate a secure escrow transaction. Funds are held safely by a licensed escrow provider until the full transfer is complete and confirmed.

FOR SELLERS
List your business and reach verified buyers
1
Create Your Listing

Our AI-guided flow walks you through everything — revenue, profit, assets, traffic, reason for selling. We help you present your business professionally to attract serious buyers.

2
Get Matched with Buyers

Your listing is surfaced to qualified buyers based on their exact criteria and budget. We handle the outreach. You only respond to serious, verified inquiries — no tire-kickers.

3
Negotiate and Agree

All negotiations happen inside Magnate. Share financials securely, sign NDAs, respond to offers, and agree on final terms — structured and documented throughout.

4
Get Paid Securely

Funds go through licensed escrow. Once the buyer confirms the transfer is complete you receive your payment. Clean, fast, and fully protected.

BUILT FOR TRUST
Verified Financials

Every listing on Magnate goes through a verification process before going live. Revenue, profit, and traffic are confirmed by our team.

Escrow Protected

Every transaction is secured through a licensed escrow provider. Funds are never held by Magnate directly.

Fast Transfers

Our structured process moves deals from first contact to closed in weeks not months. We keep both sides moving.
